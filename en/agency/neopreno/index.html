Neøpreno

Neøpreno is the music project of Juangui, raised in Madrid with roots in Colombia. Through this alias he sets out to explore every kind of genre without borders, painting atmospheres with the sounds and rhythms of the diaspora, at times including Latin or deep tribal elements.

Since 2023 he has run his own multidisciplinary art space in Viveiro, Lugo, known as "Chil na praia", where he works with DJs and artists to create an experience that lets attendees connect with the nature around them and break their chain of thoughts.
