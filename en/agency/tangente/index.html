DJ

Tangente

José Juan, a.k.a. Tangente, is a DJ raised in the lands of Seville and based in Barcelona. His sets start from bass as a core, around which IDM, Scandinavian trance, French druidstep and a pinch of jungle orbit, in broken rhythms that collapse and expand at once.

A sound in constant superposition of states that never loses the thread of its roots: the rave scene of pandemic Barcelona, carried onto the dancefloor through massive lows and relativistic BPMs that drag bodies into dancing.
