DJ · Producer

KLIM

Born and raised in Girona in a family of musicians, Climent, known as KLIM, is an artist based in Barcelona. He grew up surrounded by audio gear, spending all his free time in his father's studio, and eventually taught himself to DJ and produce.

KLIM is involved in big Barcelona parties such as No tags, 01366 and 98.3fm. He also plays top-tier streams such as those for AeAe, plus a monthly show on Hong Kong Community Radio and PNC, the London–Manchester–BCN collective 01366.
