About

ESTOCÁSTICO, CAFrom Gr. στοχαστικός stochastikós 'conjectural'.A system whose intrinsic behavior is non-deterministic.

Manifesto

Estocastic seeks to reflect this behavior in music: melodies that evolve toward unexplored horizons and unexpected shifts, transforming the energy of the dancefloor from one moment to the next. Our aim is for the listener to connect with their surroundings freely, without expectations, letting themselves be carried by the different frequencies.

We believe in the power of music to lift the spirit and go deeper into oneself. Every sound is a possibility of connection, every silence an opportunity for introspection and every transition a door to new experiences. Our intention is to create a space-time that connects consciousnesses through dance and active listening, a safe oasis for anyone who wants to connect with themselves and with others through electronic music.

Parties

Estocastic is a collective with a particular footprint in Barcelona, Valencia and Madrid. Siroco is the venue where it has settled most often during the 2025/2026 season.
